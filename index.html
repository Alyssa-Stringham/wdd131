Utah, United States of America

Utah became the 45th state in 1896. It is home to many state and national parks, forests, and monuments. Pando, a quaking aspen clone located in one of Utah's national forests, is considered the world's largest organism spanning 106 acres and weighing an estimated 13-million pounds. The world's largest natural rock span, Rainbow Bridge, is also located in Utah. Utah's state flower is the Sego Lily. Utah has an estimated population of 3.5 million people. It is the second-driest state in the nation, with an annual average rainfall of approximately 12 inches.
